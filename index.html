What's inside
✦
New tab that works

Clock, Pomodoro timer, quick links, and recent history — everything you actually need on a new tab, nothing you don't.

◈
Multi-engine search

Switch between DuckDuckGo, Bing, and Google in one click. Your choice, always visible, no menus needed.

☆
Bookmarks & history

One-click bookmarking from the address bar. History stored locally, cleared whenever you want.

◑
Light & dark mode

Both themes are designed with care. Toggle anytime from the menu — no restart, no lag.

⌥
Developer friendly

One-click DevTools for any tab. Chrome DevTools for the browser shell itself. Built for people who build things.

⊘
No telemetry

Zero analytics, zero crash reports, zero phone-home. Your browsing history stays on your machine. Always.

Built for people who find browsers exhausting

Modern browsers are feature graveyards. They track you, interrupt you, suggest things to you. Void is an Electron browser with one goal: stay out of the way. The interface is quiet. The new tab is calm. The whole thing fits in a few files you can read in an afternoon.
